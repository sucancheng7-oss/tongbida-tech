FAQ

General Frequently Asked Questions

1. What primary services does Tongbida Technology provide?

Tongbida Technology is a SaaS technical service provider focusing on comprehensive solutions for WeCom, Mini Programs, and Official Accounts. We help enterprises realize customer management, internal synergy, online store building, and efficient operation of digital business.

2. Do your systems support on-premise (private) deployment?

Yes. Beyond our out-of-the-box SaaS cloud services, we also provide complete private deployment customization for medium and large enterprises with high data security and compliance requirements.

3. How do I connect to Tongbida Technology's Mini Program system?

You simply need a verified WeChat Mini Program account and WeChat Pay merchant account, then complete authorization scanning in our backend. Our tech team will assist with basic config and code submission, usually launching within 3-5 business days.

4. If an employee resigns, will we lose their WeCom customer resources?

No. Tongbida Technology's system deeply integrates with WeCom's core APIs, supporting comprehensive "On-the-job Transfer" and "Resignation Transfer" functions. When an employee leaves, admins can reassign customers and group chats to other colleagues with one click, ensuring enterprise digital assets are secure.

5. After system activation, what post-sales tech support is provided?

We establish a dedicated post-sales service WeChat group for you, accompanied entirely by tech support and customer success managers offering 7x12 fast-response service. We also provide detailed illustrated manuals, video tutorials, and free system iteration upgrade services.
