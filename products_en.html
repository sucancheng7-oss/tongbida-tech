Tongbida Mini-Program

A suite of mini-program products designed for supply chain trading and operational synergy, building a closed loop around customer ordering, merchant operations, warehouse execution, and business follow-up. It is not just a single mall page, but an integrated system combining products, orders, inventory, procurement, customers, finance, and services.

Product Overview

One Product, Covering Trade, Inventory, Fulfillment & Operations

Tongbida Mini-Program adopts a multi-role collaborative design: the Client side handles ordering and queries, the Merchant side manages products, orders, and customers, the Warehouse side handles inbound/outbound and inventory checks, the Salesperson side handles store maintenance and sales follow-up, supported by AI Customer Service and Financial modules.

Business Flow

Tying Daily Business Processes into One Line

01 Customer Ordering

Customers search, select, add to cart, and place orders in the mini-program. Order info enters the system in real time.

02 Merchant Processing

Merchants confirm orders based on customers, pricing rules, and inventory, arranging shipping, returns, or restocking.

03 Warehouse Execution

The warehouse completes inbound, outbound, checks, and inventory change records to ensure fulfillment traceability.

04 Operational Review

Finance, customer arrears, sales performance, and rep execution flow back to management, forming an operational view.
