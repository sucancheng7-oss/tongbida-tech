What is WeCom?

WeCom is an enterprise communication and office tool built by the Tencent WeChat team. It offers a communication experience identical to WeChat, rich free OA applications, and interoperability with WeChat messages, Mini Programs, and WeChat Pay, assisting enterprises in efficient office work and management.

Through Tongbida Technology's system integration, you can deeply integrate WeCom with your business systems to achieve data interoperability.

Do I have to pay to use WeCom?

The basic functions of WeCom (such as address book management, messaging, basic OA apps, etc.) are free. However, if you need to use advanced APIs, purchase extra storage space, or buy third-party applications (like Tongbida Technology's professional apps), corresponding fees may apply.

What is the relationship between Tongbida and WeCom?

Tongbida Technology is a premium service provider for WeCom. Based on WeCom's open APIs, we provide deep customized development, system integration, and industry-specific SaaS solutions to help enterprises better utilize WeCom to manage customers and internal processes.

How do I install third-party applications?

1. Log in to the WeCom Admin Console.
2. Go to "App Management" > "Third-Party Apps".
3. Click "Add App", search in the App Market, or authorize installation via an exclusive link provided by the service provider.
4. Confirm the visibility scope and complete authorization.

How do I set up a system administrator?

In the WeCom Admin Console, Super Admins can add new administrators or modify the permission scope of existing administrators via the "My Enterprise" > "Permission Management" module, including granting them visibility and management rights for specific apps.

How do I bind my store with my WeChat Service Account?

You need to log into the Tongbida Technology Admin Console, go to "Channel Settings" > "WeChat Official Account", and click "Authorize Binding". The system will guide you to use the Official Account administrator's personal WeChat to scan the QR code and authorize. After successful authorization, your store can be accessed and provide services directly within the Service Account.
