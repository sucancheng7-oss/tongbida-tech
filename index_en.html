Why It Works

Not a single-point tool, but a business flow.

01 Online Entry

Customer Ordering

Search for products, confirm prices, and submit orders directly within the mini-program to complete the transaction entry.

Key Actions

Product selection, ordering, price checking, and order status sync.

02 Collaborative Processing

Merchant Processing

Synchronize orders, prices, inventory, and procurement arrangements, reducing manual relays and multi-system switching.

Key Actions

Order confirmation, customer segmentation, custom pricing, and procurement linkage.

03 Execution Closed Loop

Warehouse Fulfillment

Bring inbound/outbound, shipping, and exception feedback into the same business flow.

Inventory changes, warehouse-delivery coordination, and exception reporting are all recorded.

Operational Review

Precipitate accounts, customers, business execution, and service data into the same view.

Key Actions

Reconciliation, customer analysis, business follow-up, and service quality review.
